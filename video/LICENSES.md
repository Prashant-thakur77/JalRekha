# What the film is made of

| Part | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Narration | Chatterbox TTS (Resemble AI), reading in the voice of `refs/narrator.wav`, a recording supplied by the project owner (see `refs/LICENSES.md`) | Chatterbox: MIT |
| Prashant's camera lines | Recorded by Prashant for this film | Ours |
| Music | Composed for this film in `music.py`, rendered from the YDP Grand Piano SoundFont (FreePats project) | Composition: ours. SoundFont: CC BY 3.0, used as an instrument |
| Sound effects | Synthesised in `film.py` with ffmpeg | Ours |
| Screen takes | The live NadiSaathi deployment and the AWS console (read-only federated session), recorded by `record.py`; the account id is blurred and every AWS take is checked by OCR (`check_private.py`) | Ours |
| Drawn and 3D scenes | `gfx.html` (CSS) and `three.html` (three.js) | Ours; three.js MIT |
| Fonts | Host Grotesk, Fragment Mono (Google Fonts) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |

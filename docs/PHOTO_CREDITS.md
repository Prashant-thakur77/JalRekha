# Photo credits

The flood photographs used in NadiSaathi come from Wikimedia Commons. Each one is
used under the licence shown below, and each licence requires the attribution
given here. The images on the site are resized WebP copies of the originals.
Copies of CC BY-SA photos stay under the same CC BY-SA licence.

The dataset, the collection method and a reproducible download script are in
`datasets/india_flood_photos/README.md` and `scripts/fetch_india_flood_photos.py`.
Machine-readable credits are in `frontend/public/images/india/credits.json`.

## A. Alerting - Nepal

- 🟡 **np_sms_ntc_ncell**: DHM's monsoon plan says the public in affected areas is alerted by SMS from Nepal Telecom and Ncell when a river reaches or is likely to reach the red (danger) mark.
  - Quote: "general public of the concerned areas will be alerted through SMS by Nepal Telecom and Ncell"
  - Note: News report (2019) of DHM's plan; DHM site itself unreachable. Same article: 'If the water level of rivers reaches or is likely to reach the red mark'.
  - Source: [The Himalayan Times, 12 Jun 2019: DHM develops monsoon response plan](https://thehimalayantimes.com/kathmandu/dhm-develops-monsoon-response-plan/), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_ntc_1155**: Nepal Telecom gives DHM the details of subscribers in disaster-prone areas; DHM sends them early-warning SMS via the 1155 access code, free of charge.
  - Quote: "The department then sends the early warning message to those people through 1155 access code."
  - Note: Also: 'Nepal Telecom provides the details of the people living in those disaster-prone areas to DHM.' and 'Ntc has been providing all the SMS services at free of cost.' Rivers listed: Mahakali, West Seti, Kankai, Babai, Karnali, Narayani, Bagmati, Koshi.
  - Source: [NepaliTelecom: Nepal Telecom sending early warning SMS for coming natural disaster](https://nepalitelecom.com/?p=10194), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_sms_trigger**: DHM sends SMS alerts to nearby settlements as soon as a river crosses its warning or danger level.
  - Quote: "As soon as the updates show any river crossing the warning or danger level, the alerts are sent to the nearby settlements."
  - Source: [Republica: DHM sending early SMS alerts to mitigate risks of floods (dated July 25, year not shown)](https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/index.php/amp/news/dhm-sending-early-sms-alerts-to-mitigate-risks-of-floods), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_ncell_since_2016**: Ncell has sent flood early-warning SMS to people in flood-prone areas since 2016, and sent over 9 million SMS alerts in 2023.
  - Quote: "Since 2016 Ncell has been disseminating flood early warning SMS alerts to people living in flood-prone areas."
  - Note: Also: 'Last year alone in 2023, the company disseminated over 9 million SMS alerts.' A different NepaliTelecom report gives 44 lakh for 2023 (not opened); treat the count as uncertain.
  - Source: [The Himalayan Times, 11 Jan 2024: Ncell, DHM sign MoU strengthening early warning systems to cover 13 hazards](https://thehimalayantimes.com/environment/ncell-dhm-sign-mou-strengthening-early-warning-systems-to-cover-13-hazards), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_ncell_mou_2024**: Ncell and DHM signed an MoU on 10 January 2024 extending SMS early warning to 13 hydro-meteorological hazards.
  - Quote: "Flood, Flash Flood, Rainfall, Thunderstorm, Windgust, Landslide, Avalanche, Snowfall,"
  - Source: [The Himalayan Times, 11 Jan 2024: Ncell, DHM sign MoU strengthening early warning systems to cover 13 hazards](https://thehimalayantimes.com/environment/ncell-dhm-sign-mou-strengthening-early-warning-systems-to-cover-13-hazards), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_telemetry_auto_sms**: In Nepal's flood EWS, the telemetry network triggers automatic SMS to chief district officers, DHM basin offices and security forces; district offices run display boards with sirens.
  - Quote: "The second utilizes the telemetry system to trigger automatic SMS text messages, such that warnings are sent to chief district officers, DHM basin offices, along with security forces"
  - Source: [Smith, Brown & Dugar (2017), Community-based early warning systems for flood risk mitigation in Nepal, NHESS 17, 423-437](https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/17/423/2017/nhess-17-423-2017.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_cbews_gauge_readers**: Community-based EWS (CBEWS) trained community gauge readers to watch river levels and warn downstream communities when levels pass warning and danger thresholds; gauge readers warn by mobile phone and SMS.
  - Quote: "Initially the CBEWSs trained community gauge readers to monitor river levels and disseminate warnings to downstream communities when river levels rose above the defined warning and danger thresholds"
  - Source: [Smith, Brown & Dugar (2017), Community-based early warning systems for flood risk mitigation in Nepal, NHESS 17, 423-437](https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/17/423/2017/nhess-17-423-2017.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_warning_danger_def**: In Karnali CBEWS, the warning level means water reaches the top of the riverbanks at the downstream site at risk; the danger level means water overflows the banks into communities.
  - Quote: "Warning Level – condition when water reaches the top of the riverbanks for the downstream site at risk"
  - Note: Danger level: 'condition when water overflows the riverbanks and water enters the communities and floodplains downstream.' This is the paper's definition for Chisapani; DHM's own hydrology.gov.np legend could not be opened.
  - Source: [Smith, Brown & Dugar (2017), Community-based early warning systems for flood risk mitigation in Nepal, NHESS 17, 423-437](https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/17/423/2017/nhess-17-423-2017.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_dhm_stations_2014**: DHM had 286 meteorological and 170 hydrological stations (figure cited from Shrestha et al., 2014).
  - Quote: "DHM now has 286 meteorological and 170 hydrological stations nationwide (Shrestha et al., 2014)"
  - Note: Old figure (2014); current count not verified.
  - Source: [Smith, Brown & Dugar (2017), Community-based early warning systems for flood risk mitigation in Nepal, NHESS 17, 423-437](https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/17/423/2017/nhess-17-423-2017.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_dhm_sms_2021_karnali**: On 19 Oct 2021, DHM sent a mass SMS at 8 AM saying the Karnali had crossed the warning level, an hour after the gauge reader's 7 AM reading of 10.2 m at Chisapani.
  - Quote: "8 AM: SMS alert from DHM informing that warning level has been crossed in Karnali"
  - Source: [Nepal Red Cross Society et al.: Acting in anticipation against unseasonal floods in Nepal (case study, Oct 2021 Karnali floods)](https://www.dpnet.org.np/public/uploads/files/Case%20Study-%20Implementing%20Anticipatory%20Action%20in%20Nepal%202022%202022-05-06%2007-28-34.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_2026_mass_sms**: During the 26 Aug 2026 Bhotekoshi-Trishuli flood, more than 600,000 SMS warnings went to riverbank residents of Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading and Chitwan between 9:15 and 9:16 am (Kathmandu Post attributes them to the Flood Forecasting Division).
  - Quote: "More than 600,000 messages were sent."
  - Note: Mongabay reports DHM's count as 679,295 residents and says it could not verify the time; the Prime Minister told Parliament the first batch went out at 9:35 a.m.
  - Source: [The Kathmandu Post, 27 Aug 2026: How the Bhotekoshi flood unfolded over 10 hours](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/08/27/how-the-bhotekoshi-flood-unfolded-over-10-hours), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_2026_sms_criticism**: After the Aug 2026 flood, critics said official SMS warnings were generic and lacked exact locations, severity and instructions; DHM describes its system as threshold-based river sensors.
  - Quote: "threshold-based river sensors: When water crosses a preset danger level, it triggers an alert."
  - Source: [Mongabay India, Sep 2026: Community-based warning saves lives during Nepal floods](https://india.mongabay.com/2026/09/community-based-warning-saves-lives-during-nepal-floods/), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_ndrrma_act**: Nepal's DRRM Act 2074 (2017), Act No. 45, establishes the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority (NDRRMA) under the Ministry (of Home Affairs).
  - Quote: "There shall be established a National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority under the Ministry to effectively carryout and manage disaster management activities."
  - Note: English translation hosted by DPNet; assented 2074/07/05 (22 Oct 2017), first amendment 23 Feb 2019.
  - Source: [Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act, 2074 (2017), English text with Rules 2076 (copy hosted by DPNet Nepal)](https://dpnet.org.np/uploads/files/DRRM_Act_and_Regulation_english%202022-04-13%2007-11-33.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **np_ldmc**: Under the DRRM Act, every local level must set up a Local Disaster Management Committee (up to 15 members, chaired by the mayor or rural municipality chair) that develops and runs a local early-warning system and emergency operations centre.
  - Quote: "Every local level shall establish a Local Disaster Management Committee consisting of such members, not exceeding fifteen, chaired by the chairperson of village body or mayor of municipality."
  - Note: Function (j): 'Develop or cause to develop an information system on disaster management and early-warning system at local level, and make them operational;'
  - Source: [Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act, 2074 (2017), English text with Rules 2076 (copy hosted by DPNet Nepal)](https://dpnet.org.np/uploads/files/DRRM_Act_and_Regulation_english%202022-04-13%2007-11-33.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- ❌ **np_focal_person_act**: Ward or municipal 'disaster focal persons' are NOT named in the DRRM Act 2017 English text that was checked (no occurrence of 'focal').
  - Note: The NRCS case study does mention 'DRR focal person at the municipalities' as a practice, but the Act text does not. Do not attribute focal persons to the Act.
  - Source: [Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act, 2074 (2017), English text with Rules 2076 (copy hosted by DPNet Nepal)](https://dpnet.org.np/uploads/files/DRRM_Act_and_Regulation_english%202022-04-13%2007-11-33.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- ❌ **np_hydrology_portal**: hydrology.gov.np warning/danger level legend and DHM station pages could not be opened (connection refused) on the access date.
  - Note: Any claim quoting the portal's legend or per-station thresholds is unverified.
  - Source: [DHM real-time hydrology portal (connection refused on access date)](https://www.hydrology.gov.np/), accessed 2026-10-25

## A. Alerting - India

- ✅ **in_cwc_ff_stations**: CWC issues flood forecasts at 340 stations: 200 level-forecast and 140 inflow-forecast stations.
  - Quote: "Presently, flood forecasts are issued by CWC at 340 stations (140 Inflow Forecast Stations + 200 Level Forecast Stations)."
  - Note: Basin table: Ganga & tributaries 96 level + 43 inflow = 139.
  - Source: [Central Water Commission: Standard Operating Procedure for Flood Forecasting, April 2025](https://cwc.gov.in/sites/default/files/sopapril2025.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- ✅ **in_cwc_warning_level**: CWC level forecasts start when the river touches or is expected to touch the Warning Level, usually 1 m below the Danger Level.
  - Quote: "pre-defined Warning Level (usually 1 meter below the Danger Level, but also dependent on the threat perception of the particular location)"
  - Source: [Central Water Commission: Standard Operating Procedure for Flood Forecasting, April 2025](https://cwc.gov.in/sites/default/files/sopapril2025.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- ✅ **in_cwc_hfl_categories**: CWC alert categories: Above Normal (yellow) = between Warning and Danger Level; Severe (orange) = between Danger Level and HFL; Extreme (red) = above the HFL, the highest level ever recorded at the site.
  - Quote: "*Highest Flood level- The highest flood level of the river ever recorded at the place."
  - Note: Annex-3.2: 'Above Normal: Yellow', 'Severe: Orange', 'Extreme: Red'. Orange alerts updated 6-hourly, red 3-hourly.
  - Source: [Central Water Commission: Standard Operating Procedure for Flood Forecasting, April 2025](https://cwc.gov.in/sites/default/files/sopapril2025.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- ✅ **in_cwc_forecasts_per_year**: CWC issues more than 8,000 flood forecasts every flood season and has run a 7-day rainfall-runoff advisory since 2023.
  - Quote: "Annually, more than 8,000 flood forecasts are being issued by CWC during flood season."
  - Source: [Central Water Commission: Standard Operating Procedure for Flood Forecasting, April 2025](https://cwc.gov.in/sites/default/files/sopapril2025.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- ✅ **in_cwc_cap_sachet**: CWC's SOP requires CAP alerts on the SACHET platform for all level forecasts issued from divisional flood control rooms.
  - Quote: "CAP alerts should be issued for all level forecasts from DFCR in the Sachet platform"
  - Source: [Central Water Commission: Standard Operating Procedure for Flood Forecasting, April 2025](https://cwc.gov.in/sites/default/files/sopapril2025.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- ✅ **in_imd_inputs**: IMD's automatic weather stations and rain gauges, and IMD Flood Meteorological Offices, feed rainfall data into CWC flood forecasts.
  - Quote: "Automatic Weather Stations/Automatic Rain Gauges of India Meteorological Department(IMD) shall provide necessary inputs in formulation of flood forecasts."
  - Source: [Central Water Commission: Standard Operating Procedure for Flood Forecasting, April 2025](https://cwc.gov.in/sites/default/files/sopapril2025.pdf), accessed 2026-10-25
- ✅ **in_sachet_cap**: SACHET is NDMA's CAP-based Integrated Alert System, built by C-DOT, for geo-targeted alerts in local languages over SMS, Cell Broadcast, radio, TV, sirens, social media, web and apps.
  - Quote: "over all available communication media including SMS, Cell Broadcast, Radio, TV, Siren, Social Media, Web Portals and Mobile Applications"
  - Note: As of 31 Aug 2022: 'The system is already operational in 34 States and UTs. More than 75 Crores SMSes have already been sent by the system'.
  - Source: [PIB, 31 Aug 2022: C-DOT and NDMA organise All India Workshop on CAP based Integrated Alert System - SACHET](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1855808), accessed 2026-10-25
- ✅ **in_sachet_portal_header**: The SACHET portal describes itself as part of India's CAP project; SMS alerts come from the header XX-NDMAEW. Alert sources shown include IMD and CWC.
  - Quote: "User needs to be attentive when disaster alerts are received via SMS from the header XX-NDMAEW."
  - Note: Also: 'is part of CAP(Common Alerting Protocol) project implemented in India'.
  - Source: [SACHET - National Disaster Alert Portal (NDMA)](https://sachet.ndma.gov.in/), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **in_cell_broadcast_2026**: NDMA ran a nationwide Cell Broadcast alert test on 2 May 2026 through the C-DOT-built SACHET system.
  - Quote: "NDMA will test Cell Broadcast Alerts on 2 May 2026 in your area."
  - Note: News reports also cite 134 billion SMS sent through SACHET; another summary says 6,899 crore. Not verified at a primary source.
  - Source: [The Morung Express (IANS), 2 May 2026: Govt tests nationwide mobile emergency alert system](https://morungexpress.com/govt-tests-nationwide-mobile-emergency-alert-system-to-safeguard-citizens), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **in_ddma_chair**: Under the Disaster Management Act 2005 (s.25), each state sets up a District Disaster Management Authority chaired by the Collector / District Magistrate / Deputy Commissioner.
  - Quote: "the Collector or District Magistrate or Deputy Commissioner, as the case may be, of the district who shall be Chairperson,"
  - Note: Bare-act text on a third-party law site; the NDMA PDF copy is a scan that could not be text-searched.
  - Source: [Disaster Management Act, 2005, Section 25 (bare act text, AdvocateKhoj)](https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/disastermanagement/25.php), accessed 2026-10-25
- 🟡 **in_ddma_early_warning**: DM Act 2005 s.30 makes the DDMA responsible for maintaining early-warning mechanisms and getting information to the public. Section 30 does not mention control rooms.
  - Quote: "set up, maintain, review and upgrade the mechanism for early warnings and dissemination of proper information to public;"
  - Note: No source opened that ties DDMA control rooms to the Act; treat 'control rooms' as practice, not statute.
  - Source: [Disaster Management Act, 2005, Section 30 (bare act text, AdvocateKhoj)](https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/disastermanagement/30.php), accessed 2026-10-25
- ✅ **in_trai_dlt**: To send SMS over local routes in India, senders must register their use case and message templates with TRAI through a DLT portal and send with the Entity ID and Template ID.
  - Quote: "To send messages using local routes, you must first register your use case and message templates with the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) through a Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) portal."
  - Note: AWS documentation; TRAI's own site (cms.trai.gov.in) refused connections on the access date.
  - Source: [AWS End User Messaging SMS: India sender ID registration process](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registrations-sms-senderid-india.html), accessed 2026-10-25
- ✅ **in_bihar_flood_prone**: Bihar's Water Resources Department calls Bihar India's most flood-prone state, with 76% of north Bihar's population under recurring flood threat; 68,800 of 94,163 sq km (73.06%) is flood-affected.
  - Quote: "most flood-prone State, with 76 percent of the population, in the north Bihar living under the recurring threat of flood devastation"
  - Source: [WRD Bihar, Flood Management Improvement Support Centre: Flood history](https://fmiscwrdbihar.gov.in/fmis/history.html), accessed 2026-10-25
- ✅ **in_bihar_share_india**: Bihar's flood-affected area (68.80 lakh ha) is about 17.20% of India's total flood-prone area.
  - Quote: "about 17.20 percent of India’s total flood-prone area"
  - Note: The often-quoted '22.1% of India's flood-affected population' was seen only in secondary sources and is not verified here.
  - Source: [Government of Bihar, State Transformation Cell: Flood / Water Resources page](https://stc.bihar.gov.in/ShowDepartmentContentSubT.aspx?Id=MTI%3D), accessed 2026-10-25
